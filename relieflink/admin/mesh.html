How it works

Online: messages travel through the ReliefLink server in real time, with delivery ticks (✓ sent, ✓✓ delivered).
No internet: messages wait in your outbox and are sent automatically when the connection returns.
Radar: uses GPS positions to show who is within 1 km.
Note: phone Bluetooth reaches only ~10–50 m and browsers can't build a Bluetooth mesh. For true offline 1 km+ links use LoRa radios (e.g. Meshtastic).
